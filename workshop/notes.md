- 2026-10-10, the README: a tunneling spider is the hero of a rescue mission, to save the

- 2026-10-12, issue #1: the southern Bell is your wife, Isabella, and she is very Mexican.
- 2026-10-14, issue #1: the whole storyline.
  - The spider is a tunneling spider living in the sunroom of a beautiful Lake Michigan
    home, deep in its tunneled web. One day it finds three ticks placed in the web: a
    regular tick, one engorged with human blood, one engorged with dog blood.
  - The game begins with the spider's ordinary life; it doesn't know what eating each tick
    will do. Then it sees that Isabella, its love (a human, Mexican), has been captured by
    the overpopulated white-tailed deer, like Princess Peach in Super Mario. To go save
    her, it must eat its meal, then move forward.
  - Regular tick: vampiric, leeches life from enemies, very mobile, latches onto other
    animals to use as weapons ("call it mind control").
  - Human-blood tick: the blood is type 1 diabetic. The spider deals with low blood sugar
    and blurry vision; once that's dealt with, it gets psychic, spell-caster powers, like
    shooting Lyme disease at an opponent.
  - Dog-blood tick: a German shorthaired pointer's blood. Extremely athletic, hard to hit;
    dodges, and counters by "pointing", and then the attacks happen, like a hunter
    shooting where the dog points.
  - After the choice: heading from the sunroom toward the woods, the first boss is at the
    local coffee roaster: an apathetic, uncreative, self-interested roaster who attacks
    with boredom and drains your power.
  - Then a mount, to reach the woods quickly: the regular tick rides a naked mole rat, the
    human-blood tick jumps on a miniature tangerine vespa, the dog-blood tick gets the
    "zoomies" and runs at warp speed.
  - The second boss: the crumbling American middle class and high rent gouging. The way
    through is to look at who you were before the tick, be yourself and not look back,
    trusting who you've become and the journey so far. It can only be beaten with
    optimism and positive energy.
  - The last place: the cornfields up against the woods, with waves of white-tailed deer.
    The final boss is a charge of thousands of deer. The only way to beat it: release an
    egg sack of millions of baby ticks that latch onto the deer. A few "specialized baby
    tick units" attach to Isabella and keep her safe until the deer are gone.
- 2026-10-14, issue #1: you asked for it as a level-based game, seen from above like
  Pokémon Red.
