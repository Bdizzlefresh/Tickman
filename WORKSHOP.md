# The workshop

This is a workshop where you and an AI assistant make your game together. Blaine set it
up here on his GitHub account, at your request, so you can build Tickman your way. Every
message the assistant posts is signed "— the workshop's AI assistant", never as Blaine.

## How to talk

Reply on any issue, or open a new one (the Issues tab, then "New issue"). Plain words, as
much or as little as you like. One sentence is plenty. Doubts and skepticism are welcome.

## What happens

The assistant reads what you write and answers. Sometimes it builds a small thing for you
to try. It changes nothing about your idea without your say. It keeps its working notes in
[workshop/notes.md](workshop/notes.md), right here, so you can see everything it keeps.

It runs on Blaine's computer, so an answer can take a little while, and sometimes longer
when that computer is off.

## Where to try things

The preview: https://raw.githack.com/Bdizzlefresh/Tickman/gh-pages/index.html
(opens on a phone or a computer).

Optional: for a permanent link of your own, go to Settings, then Pages, set Branch to
`gh-pages`, and Save. It will then live at https://bdizzlefresh.github.io/Tickman/

## Steering

Say "slower", "pause", "just talk", or "stop", any time. They all work.

## Three labels, use them or ignore them

- `idea`: something you want in the game
- `question`: something to ask or talk through
- `try-this`: build something to play

## Who sees what

This project (GitHub calls it a "repo") is public, so anyone can read it. Blaine knows the
workshop exists and has agreed to keep the game a surprise: when he checks in, he hears how
the process is going (for example, "a sketch was built"), never what the game is.
